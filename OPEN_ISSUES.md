# OPEN ISSUES & RESOLUTION LEDGER — Thermally Coupled Reactor (TCR) Project

Tracked scientific, physical, transport, and thermodynamic issues, critique findings, and their verified resolution paths.

| Issue ID | Category | Description | Status | Resolution Path & Verification Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ISSUE-01** | Benchmark Kinetics | Khademi et al. (2009) Table 4 typo: printed $K_p = 4.89 \times 10^{35} \exp(-3190/T)\text{ Pa}^3$ has apparent activation temperature $-3190\text{ K}$, whereas $\Delta H^\circ / R \approx 205400 / 8.314 = 24704\text{ K}$. | **Resolved & Audited** | Implemented both `khademi_literal` and `khademi_thermo` in `tcr/benchmark.py`. Mathematical proof and comparison table embedded in manuscript Section 7 and test `test_t12`. |
| **ISSUE-02** | Wall Heat Transfer | The Chilton-Colburn packed bed correlation in Khademi Table 5 is for fluid-particle mass/heat transfer ($j_D, j_H$), not bed-to-wall heat transfer ($h_w$). | **Resolved & Audited** | Implemented Dixon and Li & Finlayson packed-bed-to-wall correlations for gas beds ($U \approx 200\text{–}350\text{ W/(m}^2\cdot\text{K)}$) and boiling water film coefficient ($h_{\text{cool}} = 6000\text{ W/(m}^2\cdot\text{K)}$) for CMR jacket. Verified by `test_t11`. |
| **ISSUE-03** | Thermodynamic Properties | Constant $C_p$ and constant $\Delta H_{r,298}^\circ$ cause stream enthalpy balance errors of $13\text{–}16\%$. | **Resolved & Audited** | Implemented NASA-7 / NIST polynomial $C_p(T)$ and temperature-dependent $\Delta H_r(T) = \Delta H_{r,298}^\circ + \int_{298.15}^T \Delta C_p(T')\,dT'$. Independent stream enthalpy closure error verified to $< 0.005\%$ in `test_t2`. |
| **ISSUE-04** | Shell Geometry | The $6.5\times$ area multiplier for shell flow area lacked rigorous geometric derivation and caused sub-atmospheric exit pressure. | **Resolved & Audited** | Flow area derived strictly from bundle pitch geometry without arbitrary multipliers. Shell inlet pressure elevated to $P_{\text{endo},0} = 2.5\text{ bar}$, ensuring positive discharge $P_{\text{out}} \ge 1.10\text{ bar}$ across all regimes (`test_t4`). |
| **ISSUE-05** | Fair Optimization | Unoptimized conventional reactor vs. NSGA-II optimized TCR. | **Resolved & Audited** | Formulated and solved single-objective optimization of CMR over $(T_{\text{exo},0}, T_{\text{coolant}})$, lifting CMR yield from $20.94\%$ to $30.05\%$. Syngas throughput locked strictly at $F_{\text{exo},0} = 0.640\text{ mol/s}$ across all reactors. |
| **ISSUE-06** | Le Chatelier Shell Dilution | Pure cyclohexane feed at $2.5\text{ bar}$ without inert diluent suppresses single-pass equilibrium conversion ($\Delta \nu = +3$) to $5.28\%$ at $500\text{ K}$ (a $9.4\times$ reduction vs. Khademi's 90% Ar feed). | **Resolved & Audited** | Quantified in manuscript Section 2.2.B and Table 7.1. Explained that over $88\%$ of the shell hydrocarbon acts as an internal circulating thermal fluid, necessitating downstream separation and recycling. |
| **ISSUE-07** | Optimization Objective $f_3$ Loophole | Formulating $f_3 = T_{\text{max}} - T_{\text{exo},0}$ allows optimizer to minimize $f_3 \to 0$ by raising $T_{\text{exo},0} \to 530\text{ K}$, accelerating copper catalyst sintering. | **Resolved & Audited** | Formulated objective as minimizing absolute peak temperature $T_{\text{max,tube}}$ (subject to hard constraint $T_{\text{max}} \le 535.0\text{ K}$ and $P_{\text{out}} \ge 1.10\text{ bar}$), documented in manuscript Section 4.1. |
| **ISSUE-08** | Cross-Wall Pressure Hazard | Massive differential pressure $\Delta P \approx 75\text{ bar}$ across 2962 tubes introduces catalyst poisoning hazard ($\text{CO}$ on $\text{Pt}$) upon pinhole leak. | **Resolved & Audited** | Documented in `MODELING_AND_FORMULATION_CRITIQUE.md` Pillar 1 and synthesized in AI Dossier Section 1 and Section 3 as a key operational safety constraint. |
| **ISSUE-09** | Plant-Wide Separation Fallacy | Claiming "$0.0\text{ kW}$ cooling utility" ignores downstream extractive distillation of the benzene–cyclohexane azeotrope and PSA work. | **Resolved & Audited** | Added downstream extractive distillation duty ($Q_{\text{sep}} \approx 20.0\text{ MJ/kg}_{\text{benzene}}$, $26.55\text{ MW}$) and hydrogen PSA work ($0.23\text{ MW}_e$) to Table 6.1 and README.md. Replaced "high-purity hydrogen" with "H2-rich reformate stream". |
| **ISSUE-10** | Radial Gradients & 1D Model | Low aspect ratio $D_{\text{in}}/d_p = 6.95 < 10$ and wall Biot number $Bi_h = 3.45 \gg 0.1$ induce radial temperature gradient $\Delta T_{\text{center-mean}} \approx 3.98\text{ K}$. | **Resolved & Audited** | Quantified in manuscript Section 2.1. Centerline temperatures reported across all configurations in Table 5.1. Bounded peak centerline temperature at $533.8\text{ K} \le 535.0\text{ K}$ for the NSGA-II optimum. |
| **ISSUE-11** | High-Pressure Fugacity ($K_\phi$) | Ideal gas assumption at $77\text{ bar}$ ignores polar real-gas non-idealities. | **Resolved & Audited** | Evaluated Peng–Robinson EOS at $77\text{ bar}$ and $503.15\text{ K}$, proving $K_\phi = 0.7868$ ($21.32\%$ offset in equilibrium ratio). Integrated into manuscript Section 2.2.A and Dossier Section 3. |
| **ISSUE-12** | Variable Gas Thermal Conductivity | Constant $K_{g,\text{endo}} = 0.040\text{ W/(m}\cdot\text{K)}$ ignores hydrogen enrichment ($\Delta n = +3$) which increases true $k_{\text{mix}}$ from $0.0245$ to $0.0630\text{ W/(m}\cdot\text{K)}$. | **Resolved & Audited** | Derived Wassiljewa-Mason-Saxena mixture conductivity in manuscript Section 2.2.C and Dossier Section 3. Documented local heat transfer enhancement along the bed. |
| **ISSUE-13** | Counter-Current Thermal Feedback | Counter-current TCR exhibits heat capacity flow rate ratio $\mathcal{C} = \dot{C}_{\text{shell}}/\dot{C}_{\text{tube}} = 0.361 \ll 1.0$, creating Van Heerden autothermal preheating. | **Resolved & Audited** | Quantified in manuscript Section 3.1 and Section 5.2. Explains entrance ignition and higher peak bulk temperature ($554.1\text{ K}$) in counter-current mode. |
| **ISSUE-14** | Overstated Chemical Purity Claims | Previous drafts claimed "high-purity hydrogen" and "chemical-grade benzene" straight out of the reactor shell. | **Resolved & Audited** | Replaced all occurrences with "H2-rich reformate stream" ($26.3\text{–}36.7\text{ mol}\% \text{ H}_2$) and "co-produced benzene". Enforced via automated regex gates in `check_manuscript_consistency.py`. |
